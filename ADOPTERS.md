# Armada Adopters

We're proud to see Armada being used by organizations around the world. Here are some of the organizations that have publicly shared their success stories with Armada.

Want to add your organization to this list? [Create a pull request](https://github.com/armadaproject/armada/pulls) and share your story!

| Organization                             | Success Story                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [G-Research](https://www.gresearch.com/) | G-Research is a leading quantitative research company that uses Armada in production to process millions of jobs daily across tens of thousands of nodes. |
| [APQX](https://apqx.com/) | APQX is an engineering company using Armada to schedule its large-scale simulation and machine learning workloads across the aerospace, automotive, and marine industries. |
| [NMC²](https://nmc2.com/) | NMC² (NorthMark Compute & Cloud) is a high-performance computing provider that uses Armada to schedule machine learning and simulation workloads across its GPU infrastructure. |
| [Spectro Cloud](https://www.spectrocloud.com/) | Spectro Cloud is a Kubernetes management platform that uses Armada to run customer workloads across its managed environments. |
| [StackAV](https://www.stackav.com/) | StackAV uses Armada to schedule high-throughput batch and machine learning workloads across Kubernetes clusters, supporting the compute needs of its autonomous driving development. |
